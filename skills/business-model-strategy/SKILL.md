# Business Model Strategy

Apply the [shared operating contract](../cmo/references/operating-contract.md). Read [business-model profiles](references/business-models.md) and [custom-model adaptation](references/custom-model.md) when choosing the marketing journey.

## Workflow

1. Classify transaction/revenue model, buyer/user/decision-maker, geography, stage, sales cycle, margins, capacity and constraints; keep unknowns explicit.
2. Read the business-model profiles and choose relevant patterns; combine profiles for hybrids instead of forcing one label.
3. Define the actual conversion and repeat-value event: qualified opportunity, order, booking, activated user, repeat purchase, donation or another confirmed outcome.
4. Choose channels based on buyer access, intent, purchase friction, economics and delivery capacity; include digital, partner and field routes where relevant.
5. Adapt funnel stages, asset formats, attribution windows, handoffs and success criteria to the model; test assumptions before scaling.
6. For an unfamiliar or regulated sector, use the custom-model procedure and current authoritative sources; do not claim universal domain certification.

## Deliverable

Business-model brief, buying journey, channel rationale, model-specific KPIs and adapted campaign priorities.

Read the [working reference](references/workflow.md) for relevant variants, fields and handoffs. For connected work, use [CLI capability checks](../cli-integrations/references/tool-capabilities.md); no CLI or MCP is bundled.
