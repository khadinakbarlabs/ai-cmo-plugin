# Business-model adaptation profiles

Read only the rows relevant to the business. These are starting hypotheses, not channel performance guarantees or sector certification. Industry, transaction model and business size are separate dimensions; hybrids may use multiple rows. Unknown or novel models use [custom adaptation](custom-model.md).

| Model / context | Buying/value journey | Outcomes | Channel hypotheses | Asset adaptation | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B2B enterprise/software | Buying committee; demos, security/procurement, long sales cycle | Qualified opportunity and stage progression; won/repeat value | ABM, inbound expertise, targeted outbound, partners/events | Role-specific proof, procurement brief, account play | Do not optimize raw signup volume or treat account views as pipeline |
| Self-service SaaS/product-led | Activation and recurring usage before renewal | Activation, retention, paid conversion, CAC payback with margin scope | Search/content, product-led loops, onboarding, integrations | Useful onboarding, use-case page, sharing experiment | Do not promise free trial, integrations or margins unless confirmed |
| Consumer apps/subscriptions | Store/web acquisition to repeated valuable use | Activated users, retention, paid cohorts and net revenue | ASO, paid/social, creators, referrals, lifecycle | Store metadata, creative pack, onboarding/win-back | Installs are not retained customers |
| DTC/ecommerce | Product discovery to order and repeat purchase | Qualified orders, contribution margin, repeat purchase, refunds | Shopping/search, content, creators, email/SMS with verified provider | Product/offer pack, checkout fixes, retention flows | ROAS is not profit; verify inventory, discounts and shipping |
| Retail/CPG/wholesale | Shoppers, retailers and distributors may be different buyers | Sell-through, repeat buying, distribution quality, trade economics | Retail/local, trade partners, field, brand media | Retail/trade story, activation plan, route-to-market brief | Separate sell-in from sell-through and consumer from trade incentives |
| Marketplaces/platforms | Two or more sides; activation and liquidity by segment | Match/fill rate, time-to-value, quality supply/demand, completed transactions | Side-specific acquisition, partnerships, local/network loops | Separate-side value propositions, seed-market play | Do not grow one side blindly or count listings as completed value |
| Local/appointment businesses | Catchment area and finite service capacity | Qualified booked appointments, show-up, repeat service, margin | Local SEO/maps, reviews, partners, local ads/field | Booking page, local content, review-request draft | Verify opening hours, eligibility, capacity and location claims |
| Professional services/agencies | Trust-led consultation and contract sales | Qualified consultations, accepted opportunities, won work, utilization | Thought leadership, referrals, targeted outbound, speaking | Expertise pages, proof-backed case study, diagnostic offer | Do not invent client logos, past results or service guarantees |
| Industrial/manufacturing/distribution | Technical fit, specification, RFQ, procurement and distributors | Qualified RFQs, opportunity progression, won value and repeat orders | Technical SEO/content, ABM, trade events, distributors | Specification/qualification pack, distributor/account play | Validate certifications, lead times, capability and territories |
| Healthcare/wellness | Trust, suitability and sensitive personal context | Appropriate inquiries/bookings and service outcomes defined by business | Local/search, education, professional referral, careful lifecycle | Factual service education, booking information | Verify current sector/territory rules; no invented treatment benefits or patient disclosures |
| Education/courses/coaching | Fit and enrollment to useful learner outcomes | Qualified applicants/enrollments, attendance/completion, repeat demand | Educational content, webinars, communities, partners | Curriculum/evidence pack, information session, learner journey | Do not fabricate accreditation, earnings, job-placement or testimonials |
| Finance/insurance/fintech | Trust, eligibility and regulated offers | Qualified suitable applications, activation/retention under defined constraints | Education/search, partners, selective paid, lifecycle | Eligibility/offer information, factual trust materials | Use current primary requirements; never invent returns or approval guarantees |
| Real estate/construction | Location, inventory/project fit and high-consideration decisions | Qualified inquiries, viewings/RFQs, opportunity/won value | Local/search, listings, partners, targeted outbound/field | Location/project pack, qualification and viewing/RFQ path | Verify inventory, claims and current local advertising requirements |
| Hospitality/travel/venues | Availability, geography, season, experience and booking | Qualified bookings, occupancy/utilization, cancellations, net value | Local/search, partners, creators, email, events | Availability-aware offer and booking/guest journey | Verify live rates, dates and cancellation terms; no invented scarcity |
| Restaurants/food/beverage | Catchment, convenience, occasions and repeat visits/orders | Orders/covers, repeat visits, contribution and capacity | Local/maps, social, partners, local field and loyalty | Menu/occasion content, verified offer, local activation | Verify allergens, prices, hours, capacity and offer terms |
| Media/creators/personal brands | Audience usefulness, recurring attention and paid value | Engaged returning audience, subscription/product/sponsor outcomes | Editorial/social/video, newsletter, community, collaboration | Editorial/creator pack and substantiated sponsor proposition | Separate followers/views from demand and declare paid relationships |
| Nonprofit/social enterprise | Donors, beneficiaries, volunteers and partners differ | Qualified donations, repeat donors, volunteer participation, mission-aligned outcomes | Mission content, partners, events, community, donor lifecycle | Evidence-backed impact story, donation/volunteer journeys | Do not invent impact statistics, beneficiary stories or donor restrictions |
| Franchises/multi-location | Shared brand and local economics with clear ownership | Per-location qualified demand, bookings/orders, consistency and margin | Local SEO/ads, local partners/field, central content | Central/local templates, territory/account routing | Avoid cross-location data mixing, wrong offers or assumed franchise permissions |
| Events/entertainment/sports | Tickets, sponsors, audience and event delivery | Qualified ticket sales/attendance, sponsor outcomes, repeat demand | Content, creators, partners, paid, communities, field | Ticket/sponsor assets, schedule and attendance follow-up | Verify dates, availability, rights and advertised lineup |
| Institutional/B2G/public-service suppliers | Eligibility, procurement and multiple public stakeholders | Qualified bids/opportunities, contract/service adoption and defined outcomes | Technical education, account relationships, partners, field | Evidence pack, eligibility and procurement-stage handoff | Use current tender/sector rules; do not imply privileged access or guaranteed awards |
