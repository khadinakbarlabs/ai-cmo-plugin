# Lead Generation

Apply the [shared operating contract](../../cmo/references/operating-contract.md). Use [Business Model Strategy](../../business-model-strategy/SKILL.md) to adapt goals and economics when context is missing.

Apply this reference through the owning skill; it is not a separate installed skill.

## Workflow

1. Define ideal account/customer fit, exclusions, buying roles, qualification signals and the next conversion; distinguish contacts, leads, accepted leads and opportunities.
2. Build a sourcing plan from user-owned CRM/exports, accessible relevant business evidence and exact supported owned Actors; retain source/date, confidence and contact-use status.
3. Create an offer and capture path: relevant resource, consultation, quote, demo, webinar or another supported conversion; minimize requested personal fields.
4. Specify deduplication, score components, routing owner, response SLA and CRM handoff using real available fields; do not equate an email address with a qualified lead.
5. Draft landing/form/follow-up assets and review their factual claims; keep contact evidence separate from permission to contact.
6. Measure source-to-qualified-lead, opportunity acceptance, pipeline, acquisition cost and downstream outcomes under a stated window; unknown values remain unknown.

## Deliverable

ICP and qualification rubric, source-linked prospect sheet, capture assets, routing rules and pipeline measurement plan.

Read the [working reference](lead-generation-workflow.md) for relevant variants, fields and handoffs. For connected work, use [CLI capability checks](../../cli-integrations/references/tool-capabilities.md); no CLI or MCP is bundled.
