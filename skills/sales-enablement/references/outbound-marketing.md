# Outbound Marketing

Apply the [shared operating contract](../../cmo/references/operating-contract.md). Use [Business Model Strategy](../../business-model-strategy/SKILL.md) to adapt goals and economics when context is missing.

Apply this reference through the owning skill; it is not a separate installed skill.

## Workflow

1. Define account/recipient fit, reason to contact, channel, sender identity, geography and an achievable next step; avoid treating all scraped contacts as an audience.
2. Use permitted source-linked business evidence and applicable current contact/advertising requirements; track suppression and permission/lawful-use status without declaring public contact data an opt-in.
3. Create a small personalized sequence with a genuine relevant observation, supportable value proposition, low-friction CTA and clear stop/opt-out handling.
4. Specify channel-specific deliverability, rate/volume limits, deduplication, sender/account, suppression and cross-channel contact caps; review the actual installed sending-tool contract.
5. Prepare draft/review mode by default. Send only within standing or explicit authorization for recipients, content, channel and limits; never evade platform limits or identity checks.
6. Track delivered, replied, positive reply, qualified meeting, opportunity and revenue separately; reconcile uncertain sends and stop on opt-out/complaint signals.

## Deliverable

Qualified account list, evidence-backed outreach sequence, suppression/contact rules and meeting/pipeline scorecard.
