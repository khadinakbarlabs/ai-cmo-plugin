# Outbound Marketing working contract

## Output fields

Qualified account list, evidence-backed outreach sequence, suppression/contact rules and meeting/pipeline scorecard.

Include model/audience, source dates, goal and baseline, hypotheses, ranked actions, actual assets, owners, costs/authority, measurement and unresolved inputs. Unknown data remains unknown.

## Acceptance and recovery

Select the relevant business model and buying cycle. Preserve claim evidence and meaningful conversion definitions. Produce useful drafts/imports without accounts; do not confuse a plan with connected execution. For insufficient/partial data, state sample bounds and specific unknowns. Verify account, tool and current contract before a paid or external write.

## Sequence and recovery

For each touch record channel, delay/condition, account/recipient, genuine observed context, value, CTA, opt-out and stop logic. Include sender deliverability and volume limits from the real provider; avoid invented universal safe-volume rules. Warm introduction, targeted B2B inquiry and consumer outreach have different constraints. Never silently turn a newsletter send into a prospecting campaign. On uncertain send, inspect provider IDs/events before retrying.

Use the [Resend CLI guide](../../cli-integrations/references/resend-cli.md) only if the installed provider contract and intended use allow the exact campaign; otherwise deliver drafts/import specifications.
