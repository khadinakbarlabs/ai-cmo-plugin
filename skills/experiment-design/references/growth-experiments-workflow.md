# Growth Hacking and Growth Systems working contract

## Output fields

Loop map, scored experiment backlog, first experiment pack and scale/iterate/stop decision criteria.

Include model/audience, source dates, goal and baseline, hypotheses, ranked actions, actual assets, owners, costs/authority, measurement and unresolved inputs. Unknown data remains unknown.

## Acceptance and recovery

Select the relevant business model and buying cycle. Preserve claim evidence and meaningful conversion definitions. Produce useful drafts/imports without accounts; do not confuse a plan with connected execution. For insufficient/partial data, state sample bounds and specific unknowns. Verify account, tool and current contract before a paid or external write.

## Experiment backlog

Fields: bottleneck, loop/mechanism, causal hypothesis, audience/model, baseline, estimated impact, confidence evidence, effort, cash/capacity cost, dependency, intervention, primary/guardrail metrics, sample/duration rationale, stop/rollback, owner and decision. Product-led acquisition is relevant only when the product supports the useful action. Route [experiment design](../SKILL.md) for inference; a famous tactic is not evidence of local lift.
