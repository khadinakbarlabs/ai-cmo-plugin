## Expanded business and marketing routes

- [Business Model Strategy](../../business-model-strategy/SKILL.md) (D01): Business-model brief, buying journey, channel rationale, model-specific KPIs and adapted campaign priorities.
- [Lead Generation](../../sales-enablement/references/lead-generation.md) (D11): ICP and qualification rubric, source-linked prospect sheet, capture assets, routing rules and pipeline measurement plan.
- [Inbound and Demand Generation](../../content-planning/references/inbound-marketing.md) (D07): Inbound journey, asset/distribution plan, capture/nurture pack and qualified-demand measurement.
- [Outbound Marketing](../../sales-enablement/references/outbound-marketing.md) (D11): Qualified account list, evidence-backed outreach sequence, suppression/contact rules and meeting/pipeline scorecard.
- [Account-Based Marketing](../../sales-enablement/references/account-based-marketing.md) (D11): Tiered account plan, buying-group map, role-specific assets, coordinated playbook and pipeline review.
- [Growth Hacking and Growth Systems](../../experiment-design/references/growth-experiments.md) (D10): Loop map, scored experiment backlog, first experiment pack and scale/iterate/stop decision criteria.
- [Virality and Referral Loops](../../partnerships/references/viral-marketing.md) (D08): Sharing-loop brief, creative/share assets, referral economics, instrumentation and falsifiable test plan.
- [AI Marketing](../../marketing-operations/references/ai-marketing.md) (D12): AI workflow brief, reusable prompts, reviewed pilot outputs, provider/data map and evaluation/rollout criteria.
- [Field, Events and Offline Marketing](../../launch-planning/references/field-marketing.md) (D11): Field campaign pack, logistics/creative briefs, capture/follow-up handoff and outcome/cost scorecard.

Read the [discipline map](marketing-disciplines.md) when choosing channels; read [business profiles](../../business-model-strategy/references/business-models.md) when adapting them.
