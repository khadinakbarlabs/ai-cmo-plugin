For a marketing request outside these labels, identify its intended buyer, value exchange, channel/mechanism, actual conversion, constraints and measurement. Compose the nearest useful skills and verify unfamiliar sector/channel contracts before promising execution. Do not claim comprehensive expertise or working integration for an unknown platform.
