- Metrics known / unknown and reporting window:
- Next useful action, owner, prerequisites and review date:

Do not include raw transcripts, private customer rows, credentials or full tool logs. Do not mark output accepted unless the user said so. Preserve completed campaign tasks and prior artifacts; this journal is not a replacement for the task board.
