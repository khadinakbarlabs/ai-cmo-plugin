# CMO Performance Review

Apply the [shared operating contract](../cmo/references/operating-contract.md) for evidence, local context and action scope.

## Workflow

1. Load goals, prior decisions, campaign IDs and the last comparable baseline.
2. Gather available first-party results/imports with date/filter/attribution context.
3. Separate observations, interpretations and unknowns; do not infer performance from creative presence or social views alone.
4. Review effort, cost and customer outcomes; identify a bottleneck and alternative explanation.
5. Recommend keep, change, stop or test for each priority and assign the next decision date.
6. Update the decision register and growth backlog without creating unrequested background jobs.

## Deliverable

Weekly report, evidence table, decisions and next experiments.

Use the [working reference](references/workflow.md) for covered variants, output fields and quality checks.

For account or production tools, use [CLI Integrations](../cli-integrations/SKILL.md) and its [tool capability guide](../cli-integrations/references/tool-capabilities.md). Do not assume an external CLI is installed or supports the intended action.

## Learn across sessions

Use [decision policy](../cmo/references/decision-policy.md), the latest [feedback queue](../cmo/references/feedback-loop.md) and [reporting protocol](../cmo/references/reporting-protocol.md). Evaluate prior hypotheses, actual outcomes, missing measurement and execution failures separately. Return one revised recommendation and what evidence would change it. Do not claim causality or transfer success across businesses without support.
