# Paid Media Planning working reference

## Output contract

Campaign plan, audit findings, budget scenarios and change proposal.

Record the business/campaign ID, source dates and input scope. Include the useful artifact plus evidence, unavailable fields, decision and next owner. Treat an estimated outcome as a hypothesis until measured.

## Quality checks

- Does the output answer the requested customer/business job?
- Can another person verify the important facts from the retained evidence?
- Are prerequisites and actual tool capabilities distinguished from completed actions?
- Are unknown metrics marked and recommendations tied to a test or evidence?
- Is the next handoff concrete and editable?

## Recovery

For missing tools or accounts, produce the useful draft/import-based result and identify the specific missing capability. For empty data, retain collection scope and explain the absence rather than inventing a finding. For partial results, mark the missing portion and avoid extrapolating to the whole market.

## Platform branches and economics

Choose the requested platform; verify current native requirements for Google, Meta, YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok, Microsoft, Apple Search Ads, Amazon, Reddit, Pinterest, Snapchat or X before producing a final import. Do not assume identical audience or creative fields. For local lead generation track qualified calls/bookings; for SaaS track activation and paying customers; for ecommerce track revenue with refunds and margin limitations.

Check conversion events, click-through/view-through windows and deduplication before concluding that a campaign performs poorly. Separate prospecting from retargeting, brand from nonbrand and new from existing customers. A budget model must state assumed conversion rate, unit economics and uncertainty. Pause/increase bids or budgets only through verified authorized tooling.

## Covered variants

- Audience and targeting strategy
- Campaign structure
- Budget and bidding planning
- Account audit and delivery diagnosis
- Paid search campaign preparation
- Paid social campaign preparation
- Retargeting strategy
- Campaign optimization and reporting

## Additional media and business goals

Display/programmatic, retail media, audio/podcast, connected-TV/video and native sponsorship can be planned when relevant. Research the selected placement/provider, current buying and creative specifications, audience, measurement, total cost and rights before claiming a ready import or quoting inventory. Adapt outcomes to qualified RFQs, bookings, orders, enrollments, donations or other confirmed goals; enterprise and brand campaigns can have long lagging outcomes. Do not assume a named platform CLI or direct-response attribution exists.
