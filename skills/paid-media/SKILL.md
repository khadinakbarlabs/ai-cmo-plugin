# Paid Media Planning

Apply the [shared operating contract](../cmo/references/operating-contract.md) for evidence, local context and action scope.

## Workflow

1. Define conversion, geography, economics, budget and account access; establish tracking readiness.
2. Review first-party campaign exports by objective, period and attribution window; use ad libraries only for creative research.
3. Diagnose delivery, audience, creative, landing and measurement separately; do not change all variables at once.
4. Prepare platform-appropriate structure, targeting, exclusions, creative requirements and a bounded test budget.
5. For optimization calculate valid metrics and propose concrete changes; account writes require a verified CLI and authorized scope.
6. Retain campaign/object IDs and read back changes; do not claim a campaign launched from a draft specification.

## Deliverable

Campaign plan, audit findings, budget scenarios and change proposal.

Use the [working reference](references/workflow.md) for covered variants, output fields and quality checks.

For account or production tools, use [CLI Integrations](../cli-integrations/SKILL.md) and its [tool capability guide](../cli-integrations/references/tool-capabilities.md). Do not assume an external CLI is installed or supports the intended action.
