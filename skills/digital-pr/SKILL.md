# Digital PR

Apply the [shared operating contract](../cmo/references/operating-contract.md) for evidence, local context and action scope.

## Workflow

1. Identify an audience-relevant finding, expert insight or data asset the business can substantiate.
2. Research publication fit and recent coverage with news/search tools; verify journalist/contact sources.
3. Design the asset and methodology; document limitations and fact-check claims.
4. Create a concise pitch, press materials and tailored recipient rationale.
5. Prepare follow-ups and coverage measurement; do not send unsolicited pitches or invent endorsements without authorization.

## Deliverable

PR angle, asset specification, media shortlist, pitches and measurement plan.

Use the [working reference](references/workflow.md) for covered variants, output fields and quality checks.

For optional data collection, use [Apify Research](../apify-research/SKILL.md) and its [verified Actor catalog](../apify-research/references/actor-catalog.md). Prefer the exact relevant owned Actor; imports remain useful when a tool is unavailable.
