# App Marketing and ASO

Apply the [shared operating contract](../cmo/references/operating-contract.md) for evidence, local context and action scope.

## Workflow

1. Inspect actual app listings, product value, target market and available conversion/retention data.
2. Research keyword and competitor evidence with supported tools/imports; preserve country and store distinctions.
3. Draft title/subtitle/description alternatives and screenshot storyboards within currently verified store limits.
4. Plan product-page tests, review-feedback themes and launch or paid acquisition hypotheses.
5. Measure store impression-to-install and downstream activation; hand crashes, entitlements and submission engineering to the product owner.

## Deliverable

ASO keyword/metadata plan, screenshot brief, localization notes and experiment backlog.

Use the [working reference](references/workflow.md) for covered variants, output fields and quality checks.

For optional data collection, use [Apify Research](../apify-research/SKILL.md) and its [verified Actor catalog](../apify-research/references/actor-catalog.md). Prefer the exact relevant owned Actor; imports remain useful when a tool is unavailable.
