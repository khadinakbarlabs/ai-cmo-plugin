# Field, Events and Offline Marketing

Apply the [shared operating contract](../../cmo/references/operating-contract.md). Use [Business Model Strategy](../../business-model-strategy/SKILL.md) to adapt goals and economics when context is missing.

Apply this reference through the owning skill; it is not a separate installed skill.

## Workflow

1. Choose the business outcome, location/account audience, sales journey, accessibility and confirmed event/venue constraints.
2. Compare channel fit and capacity: trade shows, customer workshops, local partners, retail activations, sponsorships, direct mail, print/outdoor or traditional media; verify current rates and schedules before quoting.
3. Create a run-of-show or placement brief, offer/proof, creative specs, staffing/fulfillment plan and contingency; vendor agreements are proposals until authorized.
4. Design minimal lead capture, permission/suppression, sales-owner routing and an actual follow-up asset using a useful value exchange.
5. Connect the physical touchpoint to attributable links, codes, booking/order records or matched account cohorts without treating scans/attendance as sales.
6. Measure attendance/reach, qualified interactions, follow-up acceptance, pipeline/order/booking/donation outcomes and total delivery cost; declare offline attribution limits.

## Deliverable

Field campaign pack, logistics/creative briefs, capture/follow-up handoff and outcome/cost scorecard.

Read the [working reference](field-marketing-workflow.md) for relevant variants, fields and handoffs. For connected work, use [CLI capability checks](../../cli-integrations/references/tool-capabilities.md); no CLI or MCP is bundled.
