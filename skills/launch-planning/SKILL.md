# Launch Planning

Apply the [shared operating contract](../cmo/references/operating-contract.md) for evidence, local context and action scope.

## Workflow

1. Define target segment, offer, readiness, date and launch success criteria.
2. Select channels, listings, advocates and events based on relevance, not maximum count.
3. Build prelaunch, launch-day and follow-up assets and dependencies with owners.
4. Verify destination-specific listing limits/rules and factual claims before finalizing materials.
5. Coordinate authorized publication and collect outcomes; preserve post-launch follow-up and learning.

## Deliverable

Launch calendar, channel/asset pack, dependencies and follow-up plan.

Use the [working reference](references/workflow.md) for covered variants, output fields and quality checks.

For optional data collection, use [Apify Research](../apify-research/SKILL.md) and its [verified Actor catalog](../apify-research/references/actor-catalog.md). Prefer the exact relevant owned Actor; imports remain useful when a tool is unavailable.
