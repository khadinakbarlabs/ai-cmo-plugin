# Keyword Research

Apply the [shared operating contract](../cmo/references/operating-contract.md) for evidence, local context and action scope.

## Workflow

1. Gather seeds from products, customer language and competitors; specify country, language and search engine.
2. Select exact owned keyword/SERP/trend Actors from the catalog and inspect their schema; use property data or imports when available.
3. Record source, observation date, units, geography and metric definition. Without a metric provider, leave volume, CPC and difficulty unknown.
4. Classify intent and relevance; group queries by shared intent and observed SERP overlap, not spelling alone.
5. Map clusters to existing/new pages; identify cannibalization and prioritize achievable opportunities.
6. Write briefs with target job, angle, evidence needs, conversion path and internal links; retain baseline.

## Deliverable

Keyword CSV, intent clusters, page map and prioritized briefs.

Use the [working reference](references/workflow.md) for covered variants, output fields and quality checks.

For optional data collection, use [Apify Research](../apify-research/SKILL.md) and its [verified Actor catalog](../apify-research/references/actor-catalog.md). Prefer the exact relevant owned Actor; imports remain useful when a tool is unavailable.
