# Customer Research

Apply the [shared operating contract](../cmo/references/operating-contract.md) for evidence, local context and action scope.

## Workflow

1. Choose research questions and a relevant, bounded sample from supplied interviews or public reviews.
2. Use review/listening Actors where relevant; retain sampling filters, dates and counts. Exclude irrelevant/private material.
3. Code jobs, triggers, objections, desired outcomes and recurring phrases; preserve exact short quotations with sources.
4. Separate complaint frequency in the sample from prevalence in the market; look for contradictory segments.
5. Translate findings into messaging, product feedback and interview questions; avoid inventing personas from sparse evidence.

## Deliverable

Customer-language bank, themes with evidence counts, objections and testable segment hypotheses.

Use the [working reference](references/workflow.md) for covered variants, output fields and quality checks.

For optional data collection, use [Apify Research](../apify-research/SKILL.md) and its [verified Actor catalog](../apify-research/references/actor-catalog.md). Prefer the exact relevant owned Actor; imports remain useful when a tool is unavailable.
