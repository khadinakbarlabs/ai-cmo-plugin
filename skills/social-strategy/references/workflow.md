# Social Strategy working reference

## Output contract

Platform-specific calendar, captions/scripts, packaging alternatives and KPI plan.

Record the business/campaign ID, source dates and input scope. Include the useful artifact plus evidence, unavailable fields, decision and next owner. Treat an estimated outcome as a hypothesis until measured.

## Quality checks

- Does the output answer the requested customer/business job?
- Can another person verify the important facts from the retained evidence?
- Are prerequisites and actual tool capabilities distinguished from completed actions?
- Are unknown metrics marked and recommendations tied to a test or evidence?
- Is the next handoff concrete and editable?

## Recovery

For missing tools or accounts, produce the useful draft/import-based result and identify the specific missing capability. For empty data, retain collection scope and explain the absence rather than inventing a finding. For partial results, mark the missing portion and avoid extrapolating to the whole market.

## Platform and advocacy variants

LinkedIn: choose personal/company voice and professional buyer context. Instagram: choose post/carousel/Reel/story with actual asset and caption limits verified. TikTok/Shorts: lead with a concrete demonstration and plan retention beats. YouTube: align title, thumbnail and content promise; analyze audience retention from actual channel exports, not public views alone. Employee advocacy: offer optional personalized drafts and truthful affiliation; do not mass-post identical messages or imply personal endorsement. Regional channels: research selected native platforms and localization needs rather than loading every region's playbook.

## Covered variants

- LinkedIn content and growth
- Instagram posts and carousels
- TikTok and short-video planning
- YouTube SEO and packaging
