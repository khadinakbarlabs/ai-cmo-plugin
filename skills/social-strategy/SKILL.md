# Social Strategy

Apply the [shared operating contract](../cmo/references/operating-contract.md) for evidence, local context and action scope.

## Workflow

1. Choose channels based on audience, objective and available creative capacity.
2. Inspect relevant content and public audience signals with supported owned Actors; separate views from business outcomes.
3. Adapt the core message to each format, including carousels, short video, captions, threads and thumbnails.
4. Build a cadence with topic, hook, asset, CTA and engagement follow-through.
5. Use social-publishing for approved execution and first-party analytics for performance; platform templates are suggestions, not universal growth guarantees.

## Deliverable

Platform-specific calendar, captions/scripts, packaging alternatives and KPI plan.

Use the [working reference](references/workflow.md) for covered variants, output fields and quality checks.

For optional data collection, use [Apify Research](../apify-research/SKILL.md) and its [verified Actor catalog](../apify-research/references/actor-catalog.md). Prefer the exact relevant owned Actor; imports remain useful when a tool is unavailable.
