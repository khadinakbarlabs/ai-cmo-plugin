# Content Production working reference

## Output contract

Editable article/guide, citations, metadata, derivatives and publishing handoff.

Record the business/campaign ID, source dates and input scope. Include the useful artifact plus evidence, unavailable fields, decision and next owner. Treat an estimated outcome as a hypothesis until measured.

## Quality checks

- Does the output answer the requested customer/business job?
- Can another person verify the important facts from the retained evidence?
- Are prerequisites and actual tool capabilities distinguished from completed actions?
- Are unknown metrics marked and recommendations tied to a test or evidence?
- Is the next handoff concrete and editable?

## Recovery

For missing tools or accounts, produce the useful draft/import-based result and identify the specific missing capability. For empty data, retain collection scope and explain the absence rather than inventing a finding. For partial results, mark the missing portion and avoid extrapolating to the whole market.

## Editorial fact checking

Keep a claims ledger: claim, evidence source/date, exact supporting passage or calculation, confidence and required qualification. Cite primary authoritative sources for material facts; a search snippet alone is weak evidence. Keep originality in the business's experience, examples and analysis rather than synonyms around competitor prose. For a comparison page, keep dated, verifiable differences and acknowledge missing data. A lead magnet or free tool needs a useful customer task, delivery format, CTA and measurement without adding an unnecessary backend.

## Covered variants

- Long-form article production
- Comparison and use-case pages
- Lead magnets and free tools
- Content repurposing

## Optional external CLI

Follow the [verified external CLI procedure](../../cli-integrations/references/wordpress-cli.md) when the task needs connected execution. Use existing accounts and the shared authority/cost contract.

## Podcasts and creator products

For a podcast, define audience promise, episode arc, guest permissions, speaking roles, intro/outro, factual source notes and clip moments. For creator revenue, compare sponsorship, subscription, affiliate and product offers against repeat demand and delivery cost; declare paid endorsements and affiliate interests. Deliver an episode pack or offer brief before connected production.
