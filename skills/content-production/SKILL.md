# Content Production

Apply the [shared operating contract](../cmo/references/operating-contract.md) for evidence, local context and action scope.

## Workflow

1. Load a brief with audience, intent, evidence, unique angle and conversion path.
2. Research authoritative sources and original business expertise; verify material claims.
3. Draft a useful structure and content, including examples and limitations; do not reproduce competitor articles.
4. Review factual support, voice, search intent, internal links and accessibility.
5. Create tailored derivatives and publish files; verify CMS writes only when specifically authorized and supported.

## Deliverable

Editable article/guide, citations, metadata, derivatives and publishing handoff.

Use the [working reference](references/workflow.md) for covered variants, output fields and quality checks.

For account or production tools, use [CLI Integrations](../cli-integrations/SKILL.md) and its [tool capability guide](../cli-integrations/references/tool-capabilities.md). Do not assume an external CLI is installed or supports the intended action.
