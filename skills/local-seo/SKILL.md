# Local SEO

Apply the [shared operating contract](../cmo/references/operating-contract.md) for evidence, local context and action scope.

## Workflow

1. Confirm service area, real locations, categories and contact information.
2. Research comparable local results and relevant reviews with owned Maps/SERP Actors.
3. Check name/address/phone consistency, service coverage, location content and legitimate review processes.
4. Write specific location/service pages and listing improvements using real evidence; avoid invented offices or testimonials.
5. Track calls, bookings and qualified local inquiries alongside visibility; distinguish recommendations from executed listing changes.

## Deliverable

Local competitor matrix, listing/location fixes, review themes and local KPI plan.

Use the [working reference](references/workflow.md) for covered variants, output fields and quality checks.

For optional data collection, use [Apify Research](../apify-research/SKILL.md) and its [verified Actor catalog](../apify-research/references/actor-catalog.md). Prefer the exact relevant owned Actor; imports remain useful when a tool is unavailable.
