# Brand and Positioning

Apply the [shared operating contract](../cmo/references/operating-contract.md) for evidence, local context and action scope.

## Workflow

1. Define the target segment, urgent job, alternatives and meaningful differentiator from evidence.
2. Build a positioning statement, value propositions and claim-evidence table; avoid claims unsupported by the business.
3. Compare pricing and packaging against willingness-to-pay evidence, costs and buyer alternatives; label hypotheses.
4. Define voice traits with before/after examples and words to prefer or avoid.
5. Review supplied assets for message consistency; propose precise revisions and tests rather than replacing an established direction.

When reviewing campaign copy, enumerate every factual, first-person, feature and offer statement. Match each to the brief/source; remove unsupported clauses directly from the asset. “Most customers I talk to” is an experience claim, not harmless filler. Include the corrected final copy and unresolved blockers, not only a general disclaimer.

## Deliverable

Positioning brief, offer/pricing hypotheses, voice examples and claim register.

Use the [working reference](references/workflow.md) for covered variants, output fields and quality checks.

For optional data collection, use [Apify Research](../apify-research/SKILL.md) and its [verified Actor catalog](../apify-research/references/actor-catalog.md). Prefer the exact relevant owned Actor; imports remain useful when a tool is unavailable.
