# AI Marketing working contract

## Output fields

AI workflow brief, reusable prompts, reviewed pilot outputs, provider/data map and evaluation/rollout criteria.

Include model/audience, source dates, goal and baseline, hypotheses, ranked actions, actual assets, owners, costs/authority, measurement and unresolved inputs. Unknown data remains unknown.

## Acceptance and recovery

Select the relevant business model and buying cycle. Preserve claim evidence and meaningful conversion definitions. Produce useful drafts/imports without accounts; do not confuse a plan with connected execution. For insufficient/partial data, state sample bounds and specific unknowns. Verify account, tool and current contract before a paid or external write.

## Pilot evaluation and AI-search distinction

An AI-assisted content/creative workflow is not the same as discoverability in AI answers. Use [GEO visibility](../../geo-visibility/SKILL.md) for answer/citation evidence and [creative production](../../creative-production/SKILL.md) for actual assets. Prompt packs should specify approved context, input boundaries, claims to verify, output structure, human owner and failure cases. Do not assume a local CLI or account connection exists in remote chat. Compare quality, revision time and cost against the manual baseline before automating recurrence.
