# Content SEO

Apply the [shared operating contract](../cmo/references/operating-contract.md) for evidence, local context and action scope.

## Workflow

1. Combine keyword intent with page inventory and available first-party performance.
2. Identify missing buying questions, overlapping pages and weak internal-link paths.
3. Use current SERPs to inspect useful formats and evidence; do not copy competitor prose.
4. For refreshes compare matching periods, filters and URLs; separate demand shifts from content defects.
5. Deliver a topical map, refresh plan, internal-link suggestions and measurable acceptance criteria.

## Deliverable

Content gap map, refresh priorities and internal-link recommendations.

Use the [working reference](references/workflow.md) for covered variants, output fields and quality checks.

For optional data collection, use [Apify Research](../apify-research/SKILL.md) and its [verified Actor catalog](../apify-research/references/actor-catalog.md). Prefer the exact relevant owned Actor; imports remain useful when a tool is unavailable.
