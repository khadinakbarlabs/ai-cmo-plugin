# Marketing Analytics working reference

## Output contract

Metric table, calculation definitions, findings, unknowns and decision-ready report.

Record the business/campaign ID, source dates and input scope. Include the useful artifact plus evidence, unavailable fields, decision and next owner. Treat an estimated outcome as a hypothesis until measured.

## Quality checks

- Does the output answer the requested customer/business job?
- Can another person verify the important facts from the retained evidence?
- Are prerequisites and actual tool capabilities distinguished from completed actions?
- Are unknown metrics marked and recommendations tied to a test or evidence?
- Is the next handoff concrete and editable?

## Recovery

For missing tools or accounts, produce the useful draft/import-based result and identify the specific missing capability. For empty data, retain collection scope and explain the absence rather than inventing a finding. For partial results, mark the missing portion and avoid extrapolating to the whole market.

## Data integrity and tracking

Keep missing/null distinct from zero and separate currency/timezone/period mismatches. Revenue should state gross/net, refund and tax treatment. CAC needs a stated acquisition-cost scope and new-customer denominator; do not silently substitute cost per lead. Conversion-rate denominator depends on the funnel step. Preserve attribution windows and models per provider. Server/browser event deduplication requires consistent event identifiers, timestamps and supported platform matching; validate actual events before calling tracking ready. Avoid exporting unnecessary personal identifiers.

## Covered variants

- Social analytics and engagement review
- Marketing baseline and KPI design
- Conversion tracking validation
- Attribution and cohort analysis
- Marketing performance reports

## Paid versus organic comparison

Compare first-party channel cohorts over the same dates, geography, currency, conversion definition and attribution window. Separate paid platform reports from analytics channel groups; do not join by vague campaign names. State spend coverage and assisted-conversion limits. Public competitor traffic estimates cannot establish their paid/organic mix or ROI.

## Profit and decisions

Revenue ROAS does not establish profitability. Contribution depends on margins, refunds, variable costs and the defined acquisition-cost scope; unknown margins mean unknown profit. A small snapshot cannot diagnose disapproval, placements or a learning period. Diagnose metadata, tracking and delivery before recommending reallocations; any benchmark or provider learning-window claim needs a current source.
