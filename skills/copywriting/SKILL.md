# Marketing Copy

Apply the [shared operating contract](../cmo/references/operating-contract.md) for evidence, local context and action scope.

## Workflow

1. Define the audience job, desired action and page/message context.
2. Build the argument from customer language, value, proof and objections; remove unsupported claims.
3. Write a clear headline, supporting message and CTA with useful alternatives.
4. Review specificity, natural voice, continuity and conversion friction; avoid generic filler and fabricated testimonials.
5. If the user has a preferred humanization CLI, inspect its contract and preserve meaning, numbers and factual support after rewriting.

Before final copy, check every offer, feature, speed, price and trial term against the business evidence. Omit unknown claims or visibly mark them for confirmation; do not silently invent a free or no-card offer. Produce a factual claim ledger; remove unsupported adjacent capabilities and fabricated founder/customer experiences from the copy itself.

## Deliverable

Editable copy, alternatives, claim notes and revision rationale.

Use the [working reference](references/workflow.md) for covered variants, output fields and quality checks.

For account or production tools, use [CLI Integrations](../cli-integrations/SKILL.md) and its [tool capability guide](../cli-integrations/references/tool-capabilities.md). Do not assume an external CLI is installed or supports the intended action.
