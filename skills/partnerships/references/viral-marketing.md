# Virality and Referral Loops

Apply the [shared operating contract](../../cmo/references/operating-contract.md). Use [Business Model Strategy](../../business-model-strategy/SKILL.md) to adapt goals and economics when context is missing.

Apply this reference through the owning skill; it is not a separate installed skill.

## Workflow

1. Choose the object people would willingly share and the recipient value: useful result, resource, identity signal, collaboration, invitation or real experience.
2. Define sender motivation, recipient activation, share moment, channel constraints and consent/privacy boundaries; a share button alone is not a loop.
3. Create original hook/content variants, share/landing experience and an optional economically supported incentive; validate incentives and fraud/refund handling.
4. Plan initial distribution through a relevant audience, creators, customers or communities with explicit authority; do not buy bot activity or manufacture endorsements.
5. Measure qualified shares/invitations, delivery, referred activation, repeat value, cost and cycle time. If computing K, state the denominator: invitations per eligible active user times invite-to-activated-user rate; deduplicate and do not call K an engagement rate. Unique invitations do not prove unique recipients or unique recipient activations; disclose those assumptions when recipient-level evidence is unavailable. Do not infer whether duplication biases a rate upward or downward from aggregate counts alone. A single cohort does not establish future constant K or decay/compounding rates.
6. Test with controlled cohorts and review retention, margin and saturation. Virality is an uncertain outcome, never a guaranteed KPI or promised campaign result.

## Deliverable

Sharing-loop brief, creative/share assets, referral economics, instrumentation and falsifiable test plan.
