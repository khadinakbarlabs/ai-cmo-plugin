# Inbound and Demand Generation working contract

## Output fields

Inbound journey, asset/distribution plan, capture/nurture pack and qualified-demand measurement.

Include model/audience, source dates, goal and baseline, hypotheses, ranked actions, actual assets, owners, costs/authority, measurement and unresolved inputs. Unknown data remains unknown.

## Acceptance and recovery

Select the relevant business model and buying cycle. Preserve claim evidence and meaningful conversion definitions. Produce useful drafts/imports without accounts; do not confuse a plan with connected execution. For insufficient/partial data, state sample bounds and specific unknowns. Verify account, tool and current contract before a paid or external write.
