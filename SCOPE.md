# AI CMO scope

AI CMO adapts marketing to the business, across models, industries, stages and organizational sizes. It covers content, ads, lead generation, inbound/outbound, ABM, growth experiments, SEO/AI-search discovery, AI-assisted marketing, virality, social/community, lifecycle, partners/PR, field/offline and measurement.

There are 39 authored skills, 140 mapped instruction workflows, 20 business profiles and a custom/hybrid adaptation procedure. The relevant funnel and primary outcome may be an opportunity/RFQ, order, booking, activated user, recurring value, donation or another business-specific event. Useful assets and measurement follow that goal.

[Business profiles](skills/business-model-strategy/references/business-models.md) · [Custom/hybrid models](skills/business-model-strategy/references/custom-model.md) · [Marketing disciplines](skills/cmo/references/marketing-disciplines.md) · [Complete workflow inventory](skills/cmo/references/workflow-catalog.csv)

A request outside existing labels uses the underlying buyer, value exchange, purchase journey, constraints and measurement to compose an appropriate workflow. Broad marketing support does not imply universal domain expertise, verified legal/sector claims, every third-party integration or proven campaign results. External execution requires actual tool/account capability and authorized scope; useful draft/import workflows remain available without accounts.

The component budget is 39 discoverable skills, 6 native Claude agents and 0 separate commands. Specialized inbound, outbound, lead generation, ABM, growth, virality, AI and field workflows are reference guides inside their owners, preserving the 140 mapped workflows. This follows a conservative local release profile rather than a verified platform maximum.

The agentic edition adds six [role playbooks](skills/cmo/references/team-manifest.json) and a [CMO coordination workflow](skills/cmo/references/agentic-workflow.md). Claude gets six native wrappers; the OpenAI adapter carries the same role guides without promising native plugin-agent registration. Independent agents run only on verified supporting hosts; other hosts get sequential role stages.
